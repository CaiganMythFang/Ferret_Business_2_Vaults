### OVERALL CONFIGS

[[black_market.json]]
Controls the base reset time of the Black Market
[[soul_shard.json]]
Controls what normal slot items (top and bottom) can show up in the black market, as well as what mobs drop soul shards when killed.
### SPECIFIC CONFIGS
#### Black Market Listings
[[omega_soul_shard.json]]
Controls what can show up in the Omega slot in Black Markets (center discounted slot).

#### Soul Diffusor
[[vault_diffuser.json]]
Controls what items can be put into the Soul Diffusor and how much Soul Dust they break down into.
