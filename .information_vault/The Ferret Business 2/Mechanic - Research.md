### OVERALL CONFIGS
[[researches.json]]
Controls what researches are available, what modID(s) are in the research, the costs, and whether all items in the mod are restricted in some way without having the Research.
[[researches_groups.json]]
Controls what Research Group each research is in, for purposes of scaling knowledge costs.
[[researches_groups_styles.json]]
Controls the visuals of each research group in the VH GUI.
[[researches_gui_styles.json]]
Controls the placement of each Research in the VH GUI.
[[skill_descriptions.json]]
Controls the text descriptions for abilities, talents, and researches.
[[skill_gates.json]]
Controls what talents and researches are gated behind others

### SPECIFIC CONFIGS

#### Mod Boxes
[[mod_box.json]]
Wold Changed. Contains the list of items that can drop from Mod Boxes, depending on which Mods are researched.
