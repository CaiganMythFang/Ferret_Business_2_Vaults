Controls what Booster Packs are available and what cards can drop from each Booster.

Cards are assembled via the booster pack as well, from the different parts handled in [[conditions.json]] and [[tasks.json]] and [[file structure/config/the_vault/card/modifiers.json]]

Card colors are declared in the code, at iskallia.vault.core.card.CardEntry$Color
