### OVERALL CONFIGS
bounty/[[bounties.json]]
Controls which types of Tasks can show up in the Bounty Table.
### SPECIFIC CONFIGS
#### Tasks
bounty/task/[[completion.json]]
Controls what Vault Completion tasks can show up on bounties.
bounty/task/[[damage_entity.json]]
Disabled. Controls what Damage Entity tasks can show up on bounties
bounty/task/[[item_discovery.json]]
Controls what Find Item tasks can show up on bounties.
bounty/task/[[item_submission.json]]
Controls what Item Submission tasks can show up on bounties.
bounty/task/[[kill_entity.json]]
Controls what Kill Entity tasks can show up on bounties.
bounty/task/[[mining.json]]
Controls what Mining tasks can show up on bounties.

#### Entities
bounty/[[entities.json]]
Controls which mobs are grouped together in relation to Kill tasks.

#### Ores
bounty/[[ores.json]]
Controls which ores are grouped together in relation to Mining tasks.

#### Rewards
bounty/[[rewards.json]]
Controls the list of rewards that can be offered on Bounties.
