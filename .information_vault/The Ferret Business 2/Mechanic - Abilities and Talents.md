### OVERALL CONFIGS
[[abilities.json]]
Controls the levels of each ability, including Over Leveling via gear.
[[abilities_descriptions.json]]
Controls the descriptions of each ability in the VH GUI.
[[abilities_gui_styles.json]]
Controls the icons and positions of each skill category in the VH GUI.
[[abilities_vignette.json]]
Controls the overlay vignette that shows up while certain skills are active.
[[skill_descriptions.json]]
Controls the text descriptions for abilities, talents, and researches.
[[skill_gates.json]]
Controls what talents and researches are gated behind others
[[talents.json]]
Controls what talents are available, how much they cost, and what level they can be purchased at.
[[talents_gui_styles.json]]
Controls the layout of available talents in the VH GUI.
