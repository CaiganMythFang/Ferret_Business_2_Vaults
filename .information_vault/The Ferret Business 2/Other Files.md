[[colors.json]]
Controls the coloring of certain values and lines in the VH GUI and other places.

[[mana.json]]
Controls the player's starting mana and mana regeneration rate.

[[menu_player_stat_description.json]]
Controls the stats listing in the VH GUI

[[points_reset.json]]
Looks like a way for an update to reset and refund abilities and talents automatically.

[[spirit.json]]
Controls how much dieing in the vault costs, calculated from the contents of your inventory and backpacks, and what you pay to retrieve your stuff.

[[trader_exclusions.json]]
Possibly modifies Wandering Trader stocks, blacklisting certain items.

[[vault_levels.json]]
Controls how much xp is needed for each level, and the max amount of levels.
